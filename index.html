🛡 Cyber Safety Tips

  • Never share your OTP, PIN or banking password.
  • Do not click unknown links sent by strangers.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for your accounts.
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible.
  • Verify unexpected payment requests independently.
  • Never install apps from untrusted sources.
  • Do not transfer money because someone threatens arrest.
